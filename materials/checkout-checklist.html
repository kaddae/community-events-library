Checkout checklist

Each time gear goes out. Amounts, care notes and tips are on the request card.

1Before they arrive

  • Pickup time confirmed by email, not just their ideal time.
  • Every item on the request card says Approved.
  • Items pulled and counted against the card.
  • Condition checked. Note anything already dinged.

2At the counter

  • Borrower agreement signed.
  • Deposit taken, if the card shows one. Write it in the desk log.
  • Chip-in taken if they offered one. “Not this time” means don’t ask.
  • Care notes walked through for each item.
  • Last host’s tip passed along for something they’re taking.
  • Drop-off day confirmed, along with the open hours.
  • Told them a “how did it go?” link comes by email after return.

3After they leave

  • Tap Check out on each item, so a late return gets flagged.
  • Send the Checkout email from the request card.
